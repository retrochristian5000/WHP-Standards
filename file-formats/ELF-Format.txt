WHP ELF Format Documentation
----------------------------

ELF Header
----------
Magic Number: 7F 45 4C 46 (UTF-8: ELF)
Endianness: See Endianness Codes
ISA: See ISA Codes

Endianness Codes
----------------
Little Endian: 01 (UTF-8: )
Big Endian: 02 (UTF-8: )

ISA Codes
---------
Generic: 00 (UTF-8: )
Intel x86: 03 (UTF-8: )
AIM PowerPC (32-bit): 14 (UTF-8: )
AIM PowerPC (64-bit): 15 (UTF-8: )
DEC Alpha: 29 (UTF-8: ))
AMD x86-64: 3E (UTF-8: >)

Software
--------
QEMU - Launches kernels in the ELF format
