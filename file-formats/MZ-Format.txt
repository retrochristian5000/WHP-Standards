WHP MZ Format Documentation
---------------------------

MZ Header
---------
Magic Number: 5A 4D (UTF-8: ZM)

MZ Variants
-----------
NE - 16-bit Windows Executable
PE - 32-bit Windows Executable

NE Extensions
-------------
Reserved (qword): 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
Reserved (20 bytes): 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
